# Code Of Conduct

This project uses the Contributor Covenant as its baseline community standard.

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project respectful, constructive, and
welcoming for people with different backgrounds, disciplines, and levels of
experience.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and precise.
- Assume good intent, but accept correction.
- Critique ideas and evidence, not people.
- Keep public threads free of sensitive material.
- Make claims proportional to proof.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment or discriminatory language
- Personal attacks
- Publishing private information or sensitive artifacts
- Posting credentials, tokens, payloads, protected evidence, or private proof
  packets
- Misrepresenting research evidence as certification or production proof
